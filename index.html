Field Tactics Builder Guide

  • Cloud Syncing Across Devices: Enter a Team Code (e.g. U13REPS) in the sidebar and tap Sync. Everything you save immediately syncs across all devices using that code.
  • Touchscreen Controls: Tap to place a player dot. Press and drag any dot with your finger to move them smoothly across the turf.
  • Sidebars on Mobile/Tablet: Tap ⚙️ Controls or 👥 Squad at the bottom of the screen to open the slide-out drawers.
  • Squad Management: Use 📝 Edit List in the squad drawer to paste your roster from WhatsApp/email.
  • Restrictions & Safety: The top-right badge audits fielders in the deep vs inner ring, and warns if any child is inside the 10m safety arc.
