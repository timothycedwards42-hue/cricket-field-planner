Tactics Builder Guide

  • 🎯 Delivery Angle & Pitch View: Click 🎯 Pitch View to set target length and line. Toggle between Over the Wicket (right of stumps) and Around the Wicket (left of stumps) to adjust the release trajectory.
  • 🟡 Fielder Shadow & Scoring Gaps: Toggle Show Scoring Gaps to visualize open boundary lanes. Fielders block scoring directly in front of them and cast realistic defense shadows all the way to the boundary fence.
  • Pitch Lengths & Lines: The shaded channels on the turf show the Off-Side Corridor of Uncertainty (amber), straight stumps (cyan), and leg side (rose), along with Full, Good, and Short length zones.
  • Placing Fielders: Select a player in the right squad panel, then tap/click on the oval to drop their dot. Jersey numbers render inside each circle.
  • Movement & Shifts: Tap the field again with the same player active to place a secondary dot, then connect them using ➔ Arrow.
  • Zen Mode: Click 📺 Zen Mode (or press `F` or `Esc`) to hide all sidebars for clean presentation during team talks. Tap ✕ Exit Zen to return.
